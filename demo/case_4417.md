# Demo case 4417 — synthetic insurance claim

**Everything here is invented for the demo.** No real person, policy or insurer. (Meta Model API terms forbid sending personal or confidential data.)

## Claim

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Claim | 4417 |
| Policyholder | "A. Example", flat 12, 1 Sample Street |
| Policy | P-88, home contents and water damage |
| Reported | 2026-10-14: water stain on the kitchen ceiling, damaged cabinet and laminate floor |
| Amount claimed | EUR 3,200 |
| Evidence | 3 photos (described in text only), plumber's note: "slow leak under the sink, joint corroded over months" |

## Policy P-88 (extract)

| Clause | Text |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | Covers sudden and accidental escape of water from fixed installations. |
| 7.2 | Excludes damage caused by gradual leakage, seepage or corrosion over a period of time. |
| 9.3 | The insurer must give the policyholder the reason for a rejection and the clause it rests on. |

## What the agent does (each step becomes a journal page)

1. **Input** — reads the claim and the evidence.
2. **Tool call** — `policy_lookup(P-88)` returns clauses 4.1 and 7.2.
3. **Tool call** — `classify_damage(plumber's note)` returns "gradual leak, corrosion".
4. **Result** — rejects the claim under clause 7.2, gives the reason (clause 9.3).

## The dispute (months later)

The policyholder says the leak was sudden. The court's expert receives pages 1, 2 and 4 and checks them offline. Muse Spark then explains the three verified pages in plain language for the judge, citing the page numbers.
